FF Research - draft room companion
==================================

1. Start the helper:   python3 draft_sync.py --room-only
2. Chrome bookmark bar -> right-click -> Add page. Name it "FF Draft".
   Paste the ENTIRE contents of bookmarklet_url.txt as the URL.
   If Chrome strips the leading 'javascript:' when you paste, type it back in.
3. Open the ESPN draft room, click the bookmark ONCE.
   A small "FF Research sink" window opens - leave it open, park it in a corner.
   A badge appears bottom-right; click the badge to stop.

The bookmarklet URL itself is in bookmarklet_url.txt (one line, no wrapping).
