FF Research — draft room companion
==================================

1. Start the helper first:   python3 draft_sync.py --room-only
   (or plain `python3 draft_sync.py` for your real draft — it uses both sources)
2. Make a new bookmark in Chrome. Name it "FF Draft". Paste EVERYTHING below the
   line as the URL. Then open your ESPN draft room and click the bookmark once.

A small badge appears bottom-right showing how many picks it has sent.
Click the badge to stop it. Re-click the bookmark to restart.
